- Milestone: M3
- Depends on: T-022, T-026

## Goal
Replace `TimetablePage`'s mock data (`INITIAL_TIMETABLES`, `DEFAULT_PERIODS` as a hardcoded constant, etc. from `frontend/src/data/timetable.ts`) with real calls to the `/periods` (T-020) and `/sections/:sectionId/timetable` (T-021/T-022) APIs.

## Technical notes
- Files: `frontend/src/components/TimetablePage.tsx`, `frontend/src/components/TimetableBreakRow.tsx`, `frontend/src/data/timetable.ts`, plus their test files.
- Fetch periods via `api.get('/periods')` instead of importing `DEFAULT_PERIODS`; fetch/write entries via `api.get`/`api.put`/`api.delete` against T-021/T-022's `/sections/:sectionId/timetable...` endpoints instead of the local `INITIAL_TIMETABLES` map.
- Subject/teacher dropdown options come from the class's assigned subjects (T-016's `/classes/:classId/subjects`), not the old `SUBJECTS`/`TEACHERS` constants.
- Role-gating of the route itself (AC-17.2) likely lives in the app's router/shell rather than this page — confirm against how `MyTimetablePage` vs `TimetablePage` are currently routed for different roles.
